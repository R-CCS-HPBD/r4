Climate Change: IPCC AR6 Key Findings Summary

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) Sixth Assessment
Report (AR6) provides the most comprehensive scientific assessment of
climate change to date.

Observed Changes:

Global Temperature:
- 1.1°C warming since pre-industrial era (1850-1900)
- Warmest period in at least 2000 years
- Last decade was the warmest on record
- Human influence is unequivocal

Extreme Events:
- Heat waves more frequent and intense
- Heavy precipitation events increasing
- Droughts more severe in some regions
- Tropical cyclones intensifying

Cryosphere:
- Arctic sea ice declining 13% per decade
- Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets losing mass
- Mountain glaciers retreating globally
- Permafrost thawing accelerating

Sea Level:
- Rising 3.7 mm/year (2006-2018)
- Faster than 20th century average
- Thermal expansion + ice melt
- Committed rise even with zero emissions

Future Projections (by 2100):

SSP1-1.9 (Very Low Emissions):
- 1.0-1.8°C warming
- Limited additional impacts
- Requires rapid decarbonization

SSP2-4.5 (Intermediate):
- 2.1-3.5°C warming
- Significant impacts on ecosystems
- Considerable adaptation needed

SSP5-8.5 (Very High Emissions):
- 3.3-5.7°C warming
- Severe and irreversible impacts
- Many tipping points crossed

Tipping Points:

Potential irreversible changes:
- Amazon rainforest dieback
- Atlantic circulation collapse
- Coral reef loss (>99% at 2°C)
- Permafrost carbon release
- West Antarctic ice sheet collapse

Carbon Budget:

To limit warming to 1.5°C:
- ~500 GtCO2 remaining (from 2020)
- ~14 years at current emission rates
- Requires 45% reduction by 2030
- Net zero by 2050

To limit warming to 2°C:
- ~1350 GtCO2 remaining
- ~35 years at current rates
- Requires 25% reduction by 2030

Solutions:

Mitigation:
- Renewable energy deployment
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Electrification of transport
- Sustainable agriculture
- Carbon capture and storage
- Reduced deforestation

Adaptation:
- Infrastructure resilience
- Early warning systems
- Ecosystem-based approaches
- Water management
- Climate-resilient agriculture

The report emphasizes that every fraction of a degree matters,
and immediate action is required to avoid the worst impacts.
