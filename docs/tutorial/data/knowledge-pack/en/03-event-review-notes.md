# Review notes — Kii Channel events (internal, 2025)

> Tutorial material: invented notes, written to look like the review notes
> a seismology group keeps next to its catalogue.

## What we checked

Travel-time residuals of regional P picks for shallow crustal events in the
Kii Channel, predicted with the global model ak135 and with our own model.

## What we found

1. **ak135 is too slow for this region.** Beyond the crossover distance the
   first arrival is Pn, and Pn arrives about **1 s early** against ak135 —
   consistent with a crust thinner than ak135's 35 km.
2. **Trade-off.** Moho depth and lower-crust velocity trade against each
   other: several model pairs fit our picks almost equally well. We chose
   the Moho at 28 km because it agrees with the receiver-function study of
   the group (internal report KRSO-RF-2024, not part of this pack).
3. **Do not over-interpret one event.** One event with a few dozen stations
   constrains an average 1-D model along those paths, not the 3-D structure.

## Decision

Adopt **KRSO-1D v2** (file 01) from 2025-04-01. Re-run all residual reports
for 2024 with v2; label old reports "v1".
